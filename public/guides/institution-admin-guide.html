Plan & Prepare

Understand your role as Institution Admin and gather what you need before you begin.

1. Introduction

About AI4I Orchestrate

AI4I Orchestrate provides a single platform for secure, controlled use of hosted AI models at scale. Organizations can offer open-source and other hosted models to institutions and their applications while retaining control over access and the underlying AI infrastructure.

It brings access controls, usage tracking, and operational monitoring together, so organizations can manage model use, measure consumption, and maintain accountability across institutions and applications.

Purpose of this Guide

Follow Part II to set up access for your institution:

  • Activate your account and sign in.
  • Onboard your applications and create API keys to give them access to models.
  • Onboard your institution's users.

After setup, use the remaining parts for ongoing tasks:

  • Part III — Track Budget & Usage: review your institution's budget and usage.
  • Part IV — Manage & Maintain: manage applications, API keys, users, and your account.

Intended Audience

This guide is for the Institution Admin responsible for managing the institution's access to AI4I Orchestrate. It covers application and user onboarding, access to models through API keys, and budget and usage tracking.

2. Before You Begin

Roles & Responsibilities

Adopter Admin — onboards your institution and assigns its tier and budget. These define the usage limits and spending cap within which you manage your institution.

Institution Admin — manages your institution's applications, creates API keys for access to models, onboards institution users, and tracks budget and usage against the assigned tier.

Institution User — is onboarded by the Institution Admin and can sign in to explore available models in the portal.

⚠DATA RESPONSIBILITY
Your institution is responsible for the data its applications collect, use, and share through AI4I Orchestrate. Follow your institution’s data-protection requirements. Confirm your deployment’s data handling, logging, and retention settings with your Adopter Admin.
ⓘNOTE
Your Adopter Admin onboards your institution, and you onboard your institution's users. Each account holder receives an activation email to verify their email address and set a password. If email is not configured for your deployment, your Adopter Admin activates your account and shares your sign-in credentials directly.

Information to Keep Ready

Keep the following information ready:

  • Access to the email address shared with your Adopter Admin. Your account activation email will be sent there.
  • Each application's name, the share of your institution's budget to allocate to it, and the permissions and expiry needed for its API key.
  • The name and email address of each institution user you want to add. Phone numbers are optional.

3. Key Terms

The following terms are used throughout this guide.

TermDefinition
Adopter

The organization that deploys and governs an AI4I Orchestrate instance. It hosts models and onboards institutions that use them.

Institution

Your organization, onboarded by the adopter to use its hosted models. It receives a tier and budget and allocates a share of that budget to its onboarded applications.

Application

An application onboarded under your institution to access hosted models. It receives a share of your institution’s budget, which can then be allocated to its API keys.

API Key

A credential that an application uses to authenticate and route requests to hosted models. Each key belongs to one onboarded application and receives a share of that application’s budget.

Service

A model made available on a particular machine or GPU. The same model hosted on another machine is a separate service.

Tier

A plan assigned to your institution that defines the quota and rate limit for each model task type. It sets how much your institution can use and how quickly it can send requests.

Budget

A monetary spending limit assigned to your institution along with its tier. It limits total spend even when quota remains.

Quota

The maximum amount of model usage your institution can consume each month, set for each model task type on its assigned tier.

Quotas support fair access to shared resources. They reset automatically at the start of each month.

Rate Limit

The maximum number of requests an institution or an API key can send to a model within an hour.

Quota limits total monthly usage. Rate limits control how quickly requests are sent, helping protect platform stability and maintain fair access.

Metering

The recording of your institution’s model usage, including the combined usage of its applications, tracked against its assigned budget.

Part II
Onboard & Provision

Sign in, give your applications access to models, and onboard your institution's users.

1. Institution Creation

ObjectiveUnderstand how your Adopter Admin sets up your institution before you sign in.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesNone. Your Adopter Admin completes this setup before you sign in.

Share the following information with your Adopter Admin. They use it to create your institution on AI4I Orchestrate and assign its tier and budget.

Information to Share with Your Adopter Admin

  • Your institution's name.
  • The name and email address of the person who will act as Institution Admin.
  • Your expected usage and spend, so the Adopter Admin can assign a suitable tier and budget.
OutcomeYour institution is created with an assigned tier and budget. These set the usage limits and spending cap for your institution and its applications.
NextYou receive an activation email when your Adopter Admin onboards your institution. Continue to Activate Your Account.

2. Activate Your Account

ObjectiveVerify your email and set your password so you can sign in to AI4I Orchestrate.
RoleInstitution Admin
Prerequisites
  • Your Adopter Admin has onboarded your institution.
  • You can access the email address shared with your Adopter Admin.
Step 1Open the activation email from AI4I Orchestrate to activate your institution and your Institution Admin account.
Step 2Click Set Your Password in the email.
Step 3Enter a password that meets the requirements shown. Enter it again in Confirm Password, then click Set Password.
Step 4Once your password is set, click Go to sign in.
Step 5Check the confirmation email sent after you set your password. It includes your institution name and sign-in email address; keep it for reference.
ⓘNOTE
If email is not configured for your deployment, you will not receive these emails. Contact your Adopter Admin to activate your account and share your sign-in credentials directly.
OutcomeYour account is activated and your password is set.
NextContinue to Signing In.

3. Signing In

ObjectiveSign in to AI4I Orchestrate using your email and the password you set.
RoleInstitution Admin
Prerequisites
  • Your institution account has been activated.
  • You have set your password.
Step 1Open the AI4I Orchestrate link shared by your Adopter Admin. Enter your email address and password, then click Sign In.
Step 2After you sign in, the Usage Dashboard opens. This is your institution's landing page.
OutcomeYou are signed in and viewing your institution's Usage Dashboard.
NextContinue to Onboard an Application.

4. Onboard an Application

ObjectiveOnboard an application under your institution and allocate a share of the institution's budget to it.
RoleInstitution Admin
Prerequisites
  • Your institution has an assigned tier and budget.
  • You know the application's name. You can also provide a description, domain, and budget allocation percentage.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Institution Management.
Step 2Click the Applications tab.
Step 3Click New Application.
Step 4Enter the Application Name. Add a Description, Domain, and Budget Allocation if needed, then click Create Application.
ⓘNOTE
Budget Allocation is optional and is a percentage of your institution's budget. Leave it blank to apply no separate spending cap to the application, or enter a percentage and adjust it later. Your institution's budget still applies.

How Budget Allocation Works

Your institution's budget is shared with its applications. Each application can then share its budget with its API keys. Each percentage is calculated from the budget directly above it:

LevelAllocationExample budget
InstitutionSpending cap assigned by your Adopter Admin₹10,000
Application70% of the institution's budget₹7,000
API key60% of the application's budget₹4,200

The amount available to allocate depends on what remains unassigned at the level above. Allocating more budget to one application leaves less for the others. The same applies to API keys within an application. When you edit an allocation, Minimum allowed shows the lowest value you can set, based on what the application or API key has already spent.

ⓘNOTE
Budget and quota are separate limits. Budget limits spend in currency; quota limits usage in units for each model task type. A request can be blocked when either limit is reached, even if the other still has capacity.
OutcomeThe application is onboarded under your institution, with a budget allocation if you entered one.
NextContinue to Create an API Key.

5. Create an API Key

ObjectiveCreate an API key that gives an onboarded application access to models.
RoleInstitution Admin
Prerequisites
  • The application is already onboarded.
  • You know the API key's required permissions, budget allocation, and expiry.
Step 1In the left navigation, click API Key Management.
Step 2Click the Create API Key tab. Enter a Key Name, select the Application, and choose the Permissions the key needs.
Step 3Enter the Budget Allocation as a percentage of the application's budget. Set the Expiry in days, then click Create API Key.
ⓘNOTE
Use a Key Name that identifies its purpose, such as Web App or Mobile App for two keys under one application. Each key belongs to the application selected when it is created and cannot be reassigned to another application.
Step 4Copy the new API key and store it securely before leaving this screen.
⚠IMPORTANT
The full API key is shown only once and cannot be retrieved later. If you lose it, create a new key.
OutcomeThe application has an API key with the permissions, budget allocation, and expiry you set.
NextContinue to Onboard Institution Users.

6. Onboard Institution Users

ObjectiveAdd users to your institution so they can sign in and test available models in Try It Now.
RoleInstitution Admin
PrerequisitesYou have the user's name and email address. A phone number is optional.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Institution Management and click the Users tab.
Step 2Click Add User.
Step 3Enter the user's Email and Full Name, and select their Role. Add a Phone Number if available. Select the consent checkbox to agree to the policies, then click Add.
ⓘNOTE
Select User for someone who needs to explore available models. Select Institution Admin for someone who needs the same administrative access as you.
Step 4The user appears with the status Pending activation. They receive an activation email to verify their email address and set their password.
ⓘNOTE
If email is not configured for your deployment, the user will not receive an activation email. Contact your Adopter Admin to activate the account and share the credentials with you directly.
OutcomeThe user is added with the selected role. Their account becomes active after they verify their email address and set their password through the activation email.
NextContinue to Part III to track your institution's budget and usage.
Part III
Track Budget & Usage

Track your institution's budget and usage against its assigned tier.

1. Usage Dashboard

ObjectiveTrack your institution's usage and spend against its assigned tier and budget, including usage by model and application.
RoleInstitution Admin
PrerequisitesYour institution has at least one application in use.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Usage Dashboard.
Step 2Click the Institution tab to review your institution's total requests, successful and failed requests, and request volume over time.
Step 3Click the Model tab to review usage and performance across your institution's models. You can see the overall success rate, the most-used model, and models ranked by request volume.
Step 4Scroll down to see the breakdown by model and service. Review total requests, consumption, and success and failure rates for each.
ⓘNOTE
One model can be available through more than one service. Each service is a deployment on a particular machine or GPU. This view groups services under the model they use.
Step 5Click the Budget tab to compare your institution's spend with its allocated budget and review usage against its assigned tier.
ⓘNOTE
The Budget tab shows all-time budget usage and cumulative usage by model task type. These figures do not change when you switch months.
Step 6Click the Applications tab to compare each application's allocated budget with its spend.
ⓘNOTE
Contact your Adopter Admin if your institution needs a different tier or a larger budget.
OutcomeYou can review your institution's overall usage, model performance, budget consumption, and application spend.
NextContinue to Part IV for day-to-day account and access management.
Part IV
Manage & Maintain

Day-to-day account, access, and testing tasks you will return to after initial setup.

1. Manage Applications

ObjectiveView your institution's applications and adjust their budget allocations.
RoleInstitution Admin
PrerequisitesYour institution has at least one onboarded application.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Institution Management.
Step 2Click the Applications tab to view your applications and their budget allocations.

Change an Application Budget

Step 3Click the Edit Budget sliders icon next to the application you want to update.
Step 4Enter the new budget percentage, then click Save changes. The allocation must stay within the amount available at the institution level and cannot fall below what the application has already spent.
ⓘNOTE
The Edit Budget window shows Minimum allowed, based on the application's spend, and the budget still Available at Institution level. Review these values before saving.

View Application Details

Step 5Click the view icon next to an application to view its budget allocation percentage and amount.
OutcomeYou can view each application's budget and save changes to its allocation.
NextContinue to Manage API Keys.

2. Manage API Keys

ObjectiveView your applications' API keys, adjust their budgets or permissions, rename them, or revoke access.
RoleInstitution Admin
PrerequisitesYou have at least one existing API key.
Step 1In the left navigation, click API Key Management.
Step 2Click the Manage API Keys tab. Use the Key Name, Application, Permission, and Status filters to find a key.

Change API Key Budgets

Step 3Click Edit Budget to adjust how an application's budget is shared among its API keys.
Step 4Select the Application and update the Budget % for each key you want to change. The amount in rupees updates automatically. Click Save changes.
ⓘNOTE
An API key's allocation must stay within the unallocated portion of its application's budget. You cannot reduce it below what the key has already spent.

Update a Key Name or Permissions

Step 5To rename an API key or change its permissions, click the edit icon next to it.
Step 6Update the Key Name or Permissions, then click Update. You cannot change the application, budget, or expiry in this window.

Revoke an API Key

Step 7To stop an API key from granting access, click the revoke icon next to it.
Step 8In the confirmation dialog, click Revoke.
⚠IMPORTANT
Revoking an API key is permanent. If the application still needs access, create a new key and update the application to use it.
OutcomeThe selected key's budget, name, or permissions are updated. If you revoked the key, it immediately stops authenticating requests.
NextContinue to Manage Institution Users.

3. Manage Institution Users

ObjectiveUpdate a user's details or role, suspend or restore their access, or delete their account.
RoleInstitution Admin
PrerequisitesYou have at least one existing institution user.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Institution Management and click the Users tab.

Edit User Details

Step 2Click the edit icon next to the user you want to update.
Step 3Update the Username, Full Name, Role, or Phone Number, then click Save.

Suspend a User

Step 4To suspend a user, click the Suspend action next to their account.
Step 5Check that the confirmation shows Suspended, then click Update.

Activate a Suspended User

Step 6To restore a suspended user's access, click the Activate action next to their account.
Step 7Check that the confirmation shows Active, then click Update.

Delete a User

Step 8To delete a user, click the Delete action next to their account.
Step 9In the confirmation dialog, click Delete.
ⓘNOTE
Suspending a user blocks their access immediately. Use Activate to restore access when needed.
⚠IMPORTANT
Deleting a user permanently removes their account and blocks their access immediately. You cannot restore a deleted user from the portal.
⚠IMPORTANT
Your institution must have at least one active Institution Admin. You cannot suspend or delete a user if this would leave the institution without an active Institution Admin.
OutcomeThe user's details or role are updated, or their account is suspended, activated, or deleted, depending on the action you completed.
NextContinue to Try It Now.

4. Try It Now

ObjectiveExplore and test an available model directly in the portal.
RoleInstitution Admin or Institution User
PrerequisitesAt least one service is available to your institution’s tier.
Step 1Open Explore and click Try it now on a model task type card. The following example uses Large Language Model (LLM).
Step 2Select an LLM Service, then choose the Source Language and Target Language.
Step 3Enter your text in Source Text, then click Translate.
OutcomeYou have submitted text to the selected model and can review its response in the portal.
NextContinue to View Available Models.

5. View Available Models

ObjectiveView the models registered on the platform by your Adopter Admin.
RoleInstitution Admin
PrerequisitesNone.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Model Management.
Step 2Review the Model Registry to see the registered models and their versions, statuses, and task types.
OutcomeYou can view the models currently registered on the platform.
NextContinue to Logs Dashboard.

6. Logs Dashboard

ObjectiveReview your institution's request logs and trace details.
RoleInstitution Admin
PrerequisitesAt least one request has been made using your institution's API keys.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Logs Dashboard.
Step 2Filter your institution’s request logs by Task Type, Status, or time range. Click the view icon next to a request to open its trace details.
Step 3Review the trace details to see the timing of each request stage, including the request, model, and inference spans.
OutcomeYou can trace your institution's requests and review their timing when investigating an application issue.
NextContinue to Manage Your Profile.

7. Manage Your Profile

ObjectiveUpdate your profile, change your password, sign out, or delete your own account.
RoleInstitution Admin
PrerequisitesNone.
Step 1Open the menu in the top-right corner and click Profile.
Step 2Review your details on the User Details tab. Click Edit to make changes.

Change Your Password

Step 3Click the Change Password tab. Enter your Current Password and New Password, then enter the new password again in Confirm New Password. Click Update Password.
ⓘNOTE
Your new password must meet every requirement shown. Each requirement turns green when it is met.

Sign Out

Step 4To sign out, open the menu in the top-right corner and click Sign out. You return to the sign-in page.

Delete Your Account

Step 5To delete your account, click the User Details tab, scroll to Delete Account, and click Delete Account.
Step 6Read the confirmation and select the acknowledgement checkboxes, then click Delete Account.
⚠IMPORTANT
You can delete your account only if your institution has another active Institution Admin. If you are the only Institution Admin, assign that role to another active user first.
OutcomeYour profile or password is updated, you are signed out, or your account is deleted, depending on the action you completed.
