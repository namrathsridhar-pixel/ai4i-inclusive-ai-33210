Adopter Admin Guide

Set up, onboard, and operate AI Switch · Version 1.0

Part I
Plan & Prepare

Understand AI Switch, and gather everything required before you begin setup.

1. Introduction

About AI Switch

AI Switch provides a single, governed layer for secure, measurable, and scalable consumption of open-source and sovereign AI models. It enables sovereign AI infrastructure to be consumed in a controlled and accountable manner while maintaining centralized governance, operational visibility, and consumption oversight across multiple institutions and applications.

Purpose of this Guide

This guide walks you through setting up AI Switch, in the order you'll typically do it:

  • Signing in to AI Switch
  • Registering models and configuring tiers and services
  • Onboarding institutions, and assigning their tiers and budgets

Part III covers monitoring platform performance and tracking budget and usage, and Part IV covers the day-to-day management and maintenance you'll return to afterward.

Intended Audience

This guide is for the Adopter Admin — the person responsible for governing AI Switch on behalf of your organization: registering models, configuring tiers and services, onboarding institutions, and monitoring platform health, budget, and usage.

2. Before You Begin

Roles & Responsibilities

Adopter Admin — you. Responsible for governing the platform: registering models, configuring tiers and services, onboarding institutions, and monitoring platform health, budget, and usage.

Adopter User — a member of your own organization, onboarded by you or self-registered, with regular user access to explore available LLMs in the portal.

Institution Admin — responsible for their own institution: creating the API keys that give their applications access to LLMs, onboarding their institution's users, and tracking their budget and usage against their assigned tier. You onboard their institution and assign the tier and budget that define what they can consume; they operate within those boundaries.

Institution User — a user onboarded by an Institution Admin, who can sign in and explore available LLMs in the portal.

ⓘNOTE
Your Adopter Admin credentials are shared with you by your Implementation team.

Information to Keep Ready

Before you begin, gather the following so you're ready to start:

  • Your sign-in credentials, shared with you by your Implementation team.
  • The models to be offered — each one hosted and reachable on your own infrastructure.
  • The tiers you'll create — the quota and rate limit intended for each.
  • The institutions to onboard — each one's organization name and primary contact (name and email; phone number is optional).
  • The tier and budget you intend to assign each institution.

3. Key Terms

The following terms are used throughout this guide.

TermDefinition
AdopterYour organization: deploys and governs this AI Switch instance. It onboards Institutions and hosts the LLMs they consume.
InstitutionAn organization onboarded by you to consume the LLMs you host. It is allocated a Tier and Budget, which it extends to the Applications it registers.
ApplicationA solution built by an institution to consume an LLM through an API Key. An institution can register one or more Applications.
API KeyA credential an institution creates, used to authenticate an Application's requests to an LLM.
ServiceA registered model version made available for consumption through a specific endpoint — the machine or GPU where that model version is hosted. The same model version hosted on a different machine is a distinct Service.
TierDefines the maximum consumption ceilings for requests and LLM usage. All Quotas and Rate Limits mapped to a Tier must stay within these ceilings. A Tier is assigned to an institution.
BudgetA monetary spend cap set for an institution, expressed in the currency defined on its assigned Tier. It governs how much the institution can spend in a billing period, regardless of how much Quota remains.
QuotaThe maximum amount of LLM usage an institution is allowed to consume within a specified time period. Quotas help ensure fair usage of shared platform resources, prevent excessive consumption, and support controlled entitlements. They reset automatically at the end of the configured time window.
Rate LimitThe maximum speed at which requests can be sent within a given period. Unlike Quotas, which control total monthly volume, Rate Limits control how fast an institution or an API Key can send requests to an LLM within an hour — protecting platform stability and ensuring fair access for all institutions.
MeteringThe recording of usage across every institution, including a rollup across each institution's Applications, tracked against its assigned Budget.
MonitoringThe ongoing tracking of system and application performance: latency, error rate, payload size, and CPU/disk utilization. Relevant to you as Adopter Admin, not to institutions.
Part II
Set Up & Onboard

Register models, configure tiers and services, and onboard institutions.

1. Getting Started

ObjectiveSign in to AI Switch and reach the platform's landing page.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesAI Switch has been deployed; Adopter Admin credentials have been created and shared with you by your Implementation team.
Step 1On the sign-in page, enter your email and password and click "Sign In." If your shared credentials use the same email as your Google account, you can also click "Sign in with Google."
The AI Switch sign-in page.
Step 2On successful sign-in, you land on the Usage Dashboard — the platform's landing page.
The Usage Dashboard, shown immediately after sign-in.
OutcomeYou're signed in and viewing the Usage Dashboard. At this stage, no models, tiers, services, institutions, or budgets have been configured yet.
NextProceed to Register a Model.

2. Register a Model

ObjectiveAdd a hosted AI model to the platform's model registry, as the first step toward making it available to institutions.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesThe model is hosted and operational on your infrastructure (hosting takes place outside AI Switch).
Step 1Click "Model Management" from the left navigation.
The Model Management area.
Step 2Click "Register Model."
The Register Model tab.
Step 3Click "Download Sample JSON" to get the template file.
Downloading the sample JSON template.
Step 4Fill in the model details in the downloaded JSON file.
The sample JSON file, completed with model details.
Step 5Click "Upload JSON File" and select your completed file.
Uploading the completed JSON file.
Step 6Click "Register Model." The platform validates the file against the required schema and shows an error if any correction is needed.
The Model Registry, showing the newly registered model.
OutcomeOnce all validations pass, you can see the model registered successfully in the Model Registry, showing its version, task type, and current status. It is not yet usable by institutions until a service is created for it.
NextProceed to Configure a Tier.

3. Configure a Tier

ObjectiveDefine a usage tier that determines the amount of LLM usage an institution is allowed.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesThe relevant LLM model is registered on the platform.
Step 1Click "Tier Management" from the left navigation.
The Tier Management area.
Step 2Click "Create Tier."
Opening the Create Tier panel.
Step 3Enter a tier name and description, and select the model task type — LLM — the unit is set automatically.
Entering tier details and selecting the model task type.
Step 4Enter the quota limit for the selected model task type.
Entering the quota limit.
Step 5Click "Create Tier."
Creating the tier.
ⓘNOTE
Rate limiting for a tier is configured separately, on the backend — not from this screen.
OutcomeThe tier appears in Tier Management, showing which model task types it covers. It is now available to be mapped when creating a service.
NextProceed to Create Service and Configure Pricing.

4. Create Service and Configure Pricing

ObjectiveMake a registered model consumable by setting its endpoint, configuring its pricing, and mapping the tier that may access it.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesThe model is registered; at least one tier exists to be mapped to this service; the endpoint where the model is hosted is reachable from the platform.
Step 1Click "Services Management" from the left navigation.
Opening Services Management.
Step 2Click "Create Service."
Opening the Create Service tab.
Step 3Select the Model Task Type and Model Name. The Model ID and Model Submission Date are filled in automatically; the Service ID is pre-filled with the model name as a prefix — complete it, and enter a Service Description.
Selecting the model and completing the Service ID and description.
Step 4Enter the Endpoint where the model is hosted. The Unit Type defaults to tokens and Currency defaults to INR for LLMs. Enter the Unit Size and Price per Unit, then map the service to the relevant Tier, and click "Create Service."
Entering the endpoint and pricing, and mapping the service to a tier.
ⓘNOTE
If you want to track usage in units (such as tokens) instead of actual cost, set the price per unit to ₹1. This makes the spend and units consumed match one-to-one in the Budget and usage dashboard.
OutcomeThe service is created for the selected model. It is unpublished by default — publish it from the Service Registry for institutions to consume.
NextProceed to Onboard an Institution.

5. Onboard an Institution

ObjectiveBring a new institution onto the platform, so its Institution Admin can sign in and get started.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesThe institution's organization name and primary contact details are available.
Step 1Click "Institution Management" from the left navigation.
The Institution Management area.
Step 2Click "Create Institution."
Opening the Create Institution form.
Step 3Fill in the institution's information — Organisation Name, Contact Name, and Email (Phone Number is optional) — then agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. These legal documents are owned and maintained by your organization.
Entering the institution details and agreeing to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
Step 4Click "Create" to create the institution.
Creating the institution.
Step 5Your tech support team activates the institution's account and shares sign-in credentials with the institution's contact.
OutcomeThe institution is active, with sign-in credentials shared directly with its contact by your tech support team. Its Institution Admin can sign in and begin providing access to applications and onboarding their institution's users.
NextProceed to Assign Tier & Allocate Budget.

6. Assign Tier & Allocate Budget to an Institution

ObjectiveGrant an onboarded institution the ability to consume LLMs, by assigning it a tier and a spending budget.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesThe institution is onboarded; at least one tier exists.
Step 1Click "Institution Management" from the left navigation.
The Institution Management area.
Step 2For the institution, click "Assign Tier."
Opening the Assign Tier panel for an institution.
Step 3Select the tier, enter the budget, and set the Effective From and Effective To dates.
Selecting the tier and entering the budget and effective dates.
Step 4Click "Assign" to assign the tier to the selected institution.
Assigning the tier to the institution.
ⓘNOTE
A tier must have at least one published service mapped to it before it can be assigned to an institution. If assignment is not available, check that a service has been created and published for this tier.
OutcomeThe institution has an assigned tier and budget. Its Institution Admin may now provide access to applications to consume LLMs, scoped to what the tier permits.
NextProceed to Part III to monitor platform performance and track usage.

The Grafana login screen.

Selecting a dashboard from the list.

The default LLM Service dashboard, with a selectable time range.
Step 5Click "Pod Resources — Service-aware" dashboard to view CPU usage, memory usage, and disk usage.
CPU, memory, and disk usage by service, on the Pod Resources dashboard.

RoleAdopter Admin

Opening the Usage Dashboard.

The Overview tab.

The generated API key, shown once.

The Manage API Keys tab, showing your organization's internal API keys.

Confirming the key revocation.
