1. Introduction

About AI4I Orchestrate

AI4I Orchestrate provides a single platform for secure, controlled use of hosted AI models at scale. Organizations can offer open-source and other hosted models to institutions and their applications while retaining control over access and the underlying AI infrastructure.

It brings access controls, usage tracking, and operational monitoring together, so organizations can manage model use, measure consumption, and maintain accountability across institutions and applications.

Purpose of this Guide

Follow Part II for initial setup. Use Parts III and IV to monitor the platform and carry out day-to-day tasks:

  • Part II — Set Up & Onboard: sign in, register models, configure tiers and services, and onboard institutions with a tier and budget.
  • Part III — Monitor & Track: monitor platform performance and track budget and usage.
  • Part IV — Manage & Maintain: manage access, update configurations, and maintain the platform after setup.

Intended Audience

This guide is for you as the Adopter Admin responsible for AI4I Orchestrate in your organization. It covers registering models, configuring tiers and services, onboarding institutions, and monitoring platform health, budgets, and usage.

2. Before You Begin

Roles & Responsibilities

Adopter Admin — you govern the platform for your organization. You register models, configure tiers and services, onboard institutions, and monitor platform health, budgets, and usage.

Adopter User — a member of your organization who can explore available models in the portal. You can add the user, or they can register themselves.

Institution Admin — creates applications and API keys for their institution, adds its users, and tracks its budget and usage against the assigned tier.

You onboard the institution and assign its tier and budget. The Institution Admin manages access and usage within those limits.

Institution User — a user added by an Institution Admin who can sign in and explore available models in the portal.

ⓘNOTE
Your Adopter Admin credentials are shared with you by your Implementation team.

Information to Keep Ready

Have the following ready before you start:

  • Your sign-in credentials, shared with you by your Implementation team.
  • The models you want to offer, each hosted and reachable on your infrastructure.
  • The quota and rate limit for each tier you plan to create.
  • The institutions to onboard, with each organization’s name and primary contact’s name and email address. A phone number is optional.
  • The tier and budget you intend to assign each institution.

3. Key Terms

The following terms are used throughout this guide.

TermDefinition
Adopter

The organization that deploys and governs an AI4I Orchestrate instance. It hosts models and onboards institutions that use them.

Institution

An organization onboarded by the adopter to use its hosted models. It receives a tier and budget and registers applications that operate within those limits.

Application

A solution built by an institution to use models through an API key. An institution can register one or more applications.

API Key

A credential an institution creates for an application to authenticate its requests to models.

Service

A registered model version made available through a specific endpoint on the machine or GPU where it is hosted. The same version hosted on another machine is a separate service.

Tier

A named set of usage limits assigned to an institution, with a quota for each model task type it covers. Rate limits for the tier are configured on the backend.

Budget

A monetary spending limit assigned to an institution along with its tier. The Effective From and Effective To dates define when it applies.

The budget limits spend independently of the institution’s remaining quota.

Quota

The maximum amount of model usage an institution can consume each month, set for each model task type on its tier.

Quotas support fair access to shared resources, prevent excessive use, and keep consumption within assigned limits. They reset automatically at the start of each month.

Rate Limit

The maximum number of requests an institution or an API key can send to a model within an hour.

Quota limits total monthly usage. Rate limits control how quickly requests are sent, helping protect platform stability and maintain fair access.

Metering

The recording of model usage across institutions, including the combined usage of each institution’s applications. Usage is tracked against the institution’s assigned budget.

Monitoring

The ongoing tracking of platform and application performance, including response time, error rates, request and response sizes, and CPU, memory, and disk usage.

Monitoring is part of the Adopter Admin’s responsibilities.

Part II
Set Up & Onboard

Register models, configure tiers and services, and onboard institutions.

1. Getting Started

ObjectiveSign in to AI4I Orchestrate and open the Usage Dashboard.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesAI4I Orchestrate has been deployed, and your Implementation team has shared your Adopter Admin credentials.
Step 1On the sign-in page, enter your email address and password, then click Sign In. If your admin account uses the same email address as your Google account, you can also click Sign in with Google.
Step 2Once you sign in, you will see the Usage Dashboard, the landing page for the admin portal.
OutcomeYou are signed in and ready to begin setup. For a new deployment, start by registering the models you want to offer, then configure the tiers, services, institutions, and budgets.
NextContinue to Register a Model.

2. Register a Model

ObjectiveRegister a hosted model so you can create a service for it.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesThe model is hosted and running on your infrastructure. Hosting is managed outside AI4I Orchestrate.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Model Management.
Step 2Click the Register Model tab.
Step 3Click Download Sample JSON. You will use this template to enter the model’s details.
Step 4Open the downloaded JSON file and fill in the model’s details.
Step 5Under Upload JSON File, click Choose File and select your completed file.
Step 6Click Register Model. The platform checks the file against the required schema and shows an error if any details need to be corrected.
OutcomeOnce validation succeeds, the model appears in the Model Registry with its version, task type, and status. To make it available to institutions, create and publish a service for it.
NextContinue to Configure a Tier.

3. Configure a Tier

ObjectiveCreate a tier that sets how much model usage an institution is allowed.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesThe relevant model is registered on the platform.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Tier Management.
Step 2Click Create Tier to open the form.
Step 3Enter a Tier Name and Description, then select the Model Task Type, such as LLM. The Unit is filled in automatically for the Model Task Type you select.
Step 4In Limit, enter the quota for the selected Model Task Type.
Step 5Click Create Tier to save the tier.
ⓘNOTE
Rate limits are configured separately on the backend. You do not set them in this form.
OutcomeThe tier appears in Tier Management with the model task types it covers. You can now select this tier when you create a service.
NextContinue to Create Service and Configure Pricing.

4. Create Service and Configure Pricing

ObjectiveCreate a service by connecting a registered model to its endpoint, setting its price, and choosing the tiers that can access it.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesThe model is registered, at least one tier exists, and the platform can reach the endpoint where the model is hosted.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Services Management.
Step 2Click the Create Service tab.
Step 3

Select the Model Task Type, such as LLM, and the Model Name. The Model ID and Model Submission Date are filled in automatically.

The Service ID starts with the model name. Complete the ID, then enter a Service Description.

Step 4

Enter the Endpoint where the model is hosted. If the model requires authentication, enter its Authentication Token. Add a Hardware Description, such as NVIDIA: RTX 3090.

The Unit Type is filled in automatically for the selected Model Task Type, such as tokens for LLM. Select the Unit Size, enter the Price per unit size, and select the Currency. The default currency is INR.

Select at least one Tier that should have access to the service, then click Create Service.

ⓘNOTE
To track usage in units, such as tokens, instead of actual cost, set both Unit Size and Price per unit size to 1,000. Spend and units consumed will then match one-to-one in the Usage Dashboard.
OutcomeThe service is created for the selected model and is unpublished by default. Publish it from the Service Registry to make it available to institutions.
NextContinue to Onboard an Institution.

5. Onboard an Institution

ObjectiveAdd an institution to the platform so its Institution Admin can activate their account and sign in.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesYou have the institution’s organization name and its primary contact’s name and email address. A phone number is optional.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Institution Management.
Step 2Click Create Institution to open the form.
Step 3Enter the Organisation, Contact Name, and Email. Add a Phone Number if available, then agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Your organization owns and maintains these documents.
Step 4Click Create to add the institution.
Step 5The institution’s contact receives an activation email. They use the link in the email to activate their account and set their password.
OutcomeThe institution is created. Once its Institution Admin completes email activation, they can sign in and onboard the institution’s users. Next, assign a tier and budget so the Institution Admin can create applications and API keys for model access.
NextContinue to Assign Tier & Allocate Budget.

6. Assign Tier & Allocate Budget to an Institution

ObjectiveAssign an institution a tier and budget so it can access models within the limits you set.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesThe institution is onboarded, and at least one tier has a published service mapped to it.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Institution Management.
Step 2Find the institution in the list. In its Actions column, click the Assign Tier icon.
Step 3Select the Tier, enter the Budget, and set the Effective From and Effective To dates.
Step 4Click Assign to save the tier and budget for this institution.
ⓘNOTE
A tier must have at least one published service mapped to it before you can assign it to an institution. If you cannot assign the tier, check that a service has been created and published for it.
OutcomeThe institution now has an assigned tier and budget. Its Institution Admin can create applications and API keys to access models within the tier’s quotas and the allocated budget.
NextContinue to Part III to monitor platform performance and track usage.
Part III
Monitor & Track

Monitor platform performance and track budget and usage across institutions.

1. Monitoring Dashboard

ObjectiveCheck platform health and performance using the Grafana dashboards provided with your deployment.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesYou have the Grafana link and your sign-in credentials.
ⓘNOTE
Your Implementation team sets up Grafana and the default dashboards, then shares the access link and sign-in credentials with you as the Adopter Admin. Additional metrics and dashboards can be configured directly in Grafana.
Step 1Open the Grafana link shared by your Implementation team. Enter your credentials, then click Log in.
Step 2Once you sign in, click Dashboards to see the available dashboards.
Step 3Click AI4ICore LLM Operations Dashboard to view latency, errors, and payload size for LLM requests across the platform.
Step 4Choose the time range and refresh interval you want to use. The dashboard shows Latency, Payload Size, Error Rate, and Error Breakdown by Status Code for the selected period.
Step 5Click Pod Resources — Service-aware to view CPU, memory, and disk usage by service.
OutcomeYou can review request performance in the LLM Operations Dashboard and resource usage in the Pod Resources dashboard.
NextContinue to the Usage Dashboard.

2. Usage Dashboard

ObjectiveReview platform-wide metrics and track usage and spending by institution, model, service, and application.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesAt least one institution with an assigned tier and budget is using a published service.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Usage Dashboard.
Step 2Start with Key metrics to see the platform-wide institution and model indicators. Use the time range and institution filter to narrow the views in the tabs below.
Step 3Click the Institution tab to review request activity across institutions: total requests, successful and failed requests, and average requests per second (RPS). Usage Concentration ranks institutions by request volume, so you can see which institutions account for the most usage.
Step 4Click the Model tab to review model usage and request performance across institutions for the selected time range. You can see the overall success rate and the most-used model. Model Consumption ranks models by request volume, helping you compare demand across models.
Step 5Scroll down to Model Consumption Drill Down to see total requests, native consumption, and success rate for each model and service. Use the Model Task Type filter to narrow the results.
ⓘNOTE
One model can be available through several services, each with its own hosted endpoint. This view groups those services under the model so you can see which services belong to it.
Step 6Click the Budget tab to compare each institution’s spend with its allocated budget. Each row also shows the institution’s assigned tier and the amount still available. Use Filter by tier to view institutions on a particular tier. Institutions that have exceeded their budget are flagged.
Step 7Click the Applications tab to view usage by application across institutions.
OutcomeYou can review platform-wide activity in Key metrics, compare usage in the Institution and Model tabs, check spending in the Budget tab, and view application usage in the Applications tab.
NextContinue to Part IV for day-to-day management and maintenance.
Part IV
Manage & Maintain

Manage your institutions, applications, API keys, tiers, models, services, users, and profile.

1. Manage Institutions

ObjectiveSuspend an institution, reactivate a suspended institution, or deactivate it.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesThe institution is already onboarded.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Institution Management.
Step 2Find the institution you want to suspend, then click Suspend.
Step 3In the confirmation dialog, click Update to suspend the institution.
⚠IMPORTANT
Suspending an institution immediately makes its API keys inactive and blocks requests from its applications. Its Institution Admin and users can no longer sign in to the portal.
Step 4To reactivate a suspended institution, click Activate next to it.
Step 5In the confirmation dialog, click Update to reactivate the institution.
ⓘNOTE
When you reactivate a suspended institution, its existing API keys automatically return to Active.
Step 6To deactivate an institution, click Deactivate next to it.
Step 7In the confirmation dialog, click Update to deactivate the institution.
⚠IMPORTANT
Deactivating an institution revokes its API keys. If you reactivate the institution later, its Institution Admin must create new keys before its applications can access models again.
OutcomeThe institution’s status is updated to reflect the action you confirmed.
NextContinue to Create & Manage Applications.

2. Create & Manage Applications

ObjectiveRegister an application under your organization and manage its share of your organization’s budget.
RoleAdopter Admin
Prerequisites
  • Your organization has an assigned tier and budget.
  • You have the application’s name. You can also provide a description, domain, and budget allocation percentage.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Institution Management. Then click the view icon for your organization, shown as default organisation.
Step 2Click the Applications tab to see your organization’s applications and budget allocation.
Step 3Click New Application to open the form.
Step 4Enter the Application Name. Add a Description, Domain, and Budget Allocation if needed, then click Create Application.
ⓘNOTE
Budget Allocation is optional and is entered as a percentage of your organization’s budget. Leave it blank if you do not want to set a separate spending ceiling for the application. You can set or change the allocation later.
Step 5To change an application’s budget allocation, click the Edit Budget sliders icon, next to the application.
Step 6Enter the new percentage, then click Save changes. The allocation must stay within the budget available at the institution level and cannot be reduced below what the application has already spent.
ⓘNOTE
The dialog shows Institution Budget Allocated and Available at Institution level, so you can see how much has been allocated and how much remains before saving the change.
Step 7To view an application’s current allocation, click the view icon next to it. You can see both the budget percentage and the corresponding amount.
OutcomeThe application is registered under your organization. You can view its budget allocation and adjust it as needed.
NextContinue to Create & Manage API Keys.

3. Create & Manage API Keys

ObjectiveCreate API keys for your organization’s applications, update their details, adjust their budgets, or revoke them.
RoleAdopter Admin
Prerequisites
  • The application that will use the key has been created.
  • You have decided the key’s permissions, budget allocation, and expiry.
Step 1In the left navigation, click API Key Management.
Step 2Click the Create API Key tab.
Step 3Enter a Key Name, select the Application that will use the key, and choose the Permissions it needs.
Step 4Enter the Budget Allocation as a percentage of the application’s budget. Set the Expiry in days, then click Create API Key.
Step 5Copy the generated key and store it securely before leaving this screen. The full key is shown only once.
⚠IMPORTANT
You cannot retrieve the full key again after leaving this screen. If you lose it, revoke the key and create a new one.
Step 6Click the Manage API Keys tab to view your existing keys. Use the Key Name, Application, Permission, and Status filters to find the keys you need.
Step 7To adjust the budget allocated to an application’s existing keys, click Edit Budget.
Step 8Select the Application, then change Budget % for the keys you want to update. Budget (INR) updates automatically as you change the percentage. Click Save changes when you are done.
ⓘNOTE
Only the keys you edit are updated when you save. Other keys keep their current budgets. You can leave part of the application’s budget unallocated; the total does not have to reach 100%.
Step 9To rename a key or change its permissions, click the edit icon next to it.
Step 10Update Key Name or Permissions, then click Update. You cannot change the key’s application, budget, or expiry in this dialog.
Step 11To revoke a key, click the revoke icon next to it.
Step 12In the confirmation dialog, click Revoke.
⚠IMPORTANT
Revoking a key is permanent. A revoked key cannot be reactivated. If the application still needs access, create a new key and update the application to use it.
OutcomeA newly created key is ready to authenticate requests. Budget changes and edits are saved to the selected keys. If you revoke a key, it stops authenticating requests immediately.
NextContinue to Manage Tiers.

4. Manage Tiers

ObjectiveView a tier, update its details, schedule a quota change, or delete it.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesAt least one tier exists.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Tier Management.
Step 2To view a tier’s details, click the view icon next to it.
Step 3To change the tier’s name, description, or quota, click the edit icon next to it.
Step 4Update the Tier Name, Description, or quota limit, then click Save Changes.
Step 5To schedule a quota change, click the schedule icon next to the quota. Enter the new limit, then click Confirm Schedule.
ⓘNOTE
A scheduled quota change takes effect at the start of the next month.
Step 6To delete a tier, click the delete icon next to it.
Step 7In the confirmation dialog, click Delete.
⚠IMPORTANT
You cannot delete a tier while it is mapped to a service or assigned to an institution. Unmap the tier from its services and remove or change the institution’s tier assignment before deleting the tier.
OutcomeYour changes are saved to the tier, a quota change is scheduled, or the tier is deleted, depending on the action you took.
NextContinue to Manage Models.

5. Manage Models

ObjectiveCheck a registered model’s status, deprecate it, or reactivate a deprecated model.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesAt least one model is registered.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Model Management.
Step 2In the Model Registry, you can see each model’s version, task type, and status.
Step 3To deprecate a model, click the toggle next to it.
Step 4In the confirmation dialog, click Confirm.
⚠IMPORTANT
You cannot deprecate a model while any of its services are published. Unpublish every service linked to the model first.
Step 5To reactivate a deprecated model, click the toggle next to it.
Step 6In the confirmation dialog, click Confirm.
OutcomeThe Model Registry shows the model’s updated status as deprecated or active.
NextContinue to Manage Services.

6. Manage Services

ObjectiveView a service, edit its settings, publish or unpublish it, or delete it.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesAt least one service exists.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Services Management.
Step 2In the Service Registry, click the view icon next to a service to see its configuration.
Step 3To change the service’s settings, click the edit icon next to it.
Step 4Update the endpoint, pricing, or mapped tiers as needed, then click Save Changes.
Step 5To unpublish a service, click the unpublish icon next to it.
Step 6In the confirmation dialog, click Confirm to unpublish the service.
⚠IMPORTANT
Unpublishing a service immediately stops access for institutions and their applications. They can use it again once it is published.
Step 7To publish an unpublished service, click the publish icon next to it.
Step 8In the confirmation dialog, click Confirm to publish the service.
Step 9To delete a service, click the delete icon next to it.
ⓘNOTE
Unpublish a service before deleting it. Deletion cannot be undone.
OutcomeYour changes are saved to the service. Publishing makes it available for access; unpublishing stops access. If you delete the service, it is removed permanently.
NextContinue to Onboard Your Organization’s Users.

7. Onboard Your Organization's Users

ObjectiveAdd users to your own organization, or let them register themselves.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesNone.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Institution Management.
Step 2Click the view icon for your organization, shown as default organisation, then click Add User.
Step 3Enter the user’s Email and Full Name, select their Role, and add a Phone Number if available. Agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy on their behalf, then click Add.
ⓘNOTE
The user receives an email to register their account and set a password. Once they complete these steps, their account is activated.

If the user is registering their own account, they can follow the sign-up step below.

Step 4The user opens the Sign-Up page, enters their full name, email address, and password, and agrees to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to create their account.
ⓘNOTE
The user receives an activation email. Once they verify their email address, their account is activated and they can sign in with the password they set during sign-up.

To assign or change a user’s role after registration, follow Manage User Roles in Manage Your Profile.

OutcomeThe user’s account is activated once they complete the steps in their email. They can then sign in with the role assigned to them, whether you added the user or they registered themselves.
NextContinue to Manage Your Profile.

8. Manage Your Profile

ObjectiveUpdate your profile details, change your password, manage user roles, or sign out.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesNone.
Step 1Open the menu in the top-right corner, then click Profile.
Step 2On the Profile page, view or edit your user details.
Step 3To change your password, click the Change Password tab. Enter your Current Password and New Password, then enter the new password again in Confirm New Password. Click Update Password.
ⓘNOTE
Your new password must meet every requirement shown on the screen. Each requirement turns green when it is satisfied.

Manage User Roles

Step 4To assign or change a user’s role, click the Roles tab on the Profile page.
Step 5Select the user from the dropdown, then click Manage Roles.
Step 6Choose the role you want to assign, then click Save Changes.
ⓘNOTE
A user can hold more than one role on the platform.

Sign Out

Step 7To end your current session, open the menu in the top-right corner and click Sign out.
OutcomeYour profile details, password, or user roles are updated. If you sign out, your current session ends.
NextContinue to the Logs Dashboard.

9. Logs Dashboard

ObjectiveReview requests across institutions and open a request’s trace to see its details.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesAt least one request has been made on the platform.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Logs Dashboard.
Step 2You can see total, successful, and failed requests. Filter by Institution, Task Type, Status, or time range, then click the view icon next to a request to open its trace.
Step 3Review the trace details and the individual spans for the selected request.
OutcomeYou can review requests across the platform and use the filters to find and inspect a specific request.
NextContinue to Try It Now.

10. Try It Now

ObjectiveExplore the available model task types and try a service directly in the portal.
RoleAdopter Admin
PrerequisitesAt least one service is published.
Step 1In the left navigation, click Explore to open the page showing the available model task types.
Step 2Click Try it now on the task type you want to test. For this example, choose Large Language Model (LLM).
Step 3Select an available service from the LLM Service dropdown. The service name and description appear below it.
Step 4Choose the Source Language and Target Language, then enter your text in Source Text. For example, select English and Hindi and enter “Hi how are you”. Click Translate to run the request.
OutcomeYou can test a model through its available service directly in the portal. This example shows an LLM service translating text from English to Hindi.
